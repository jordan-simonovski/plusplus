`/plusplus-settings` is now a proper settings panel. Pick where replies go, set the snark level and change karma reactions right on the panel, with no commands to remember: choose a tier, type the emojis and press Enter. Workspace admins can tick a box to change reactions for the whole workspace. The panel updates in place, so it always shows the channel's current settings and what just changed.
