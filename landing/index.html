How it works

No setup, no syntax to memorize. React or type like you already do.

  1. 🔥

    React to award

    React to someone's message and they get karma. Stronger emojis give more: 👏 +1, 🙌 +2, ⭐ +3, 🔥 +4, 🏆 +5. Changed your mind? Remove the reaction and the karma goes back.

  2. 🗑️

    React to dock

    Same idea in reverse: 🙄 −1, 👎 −2, 🤡 −3, 💩 −4, 🗑️ −5. Every channel can swap in its own emojis, so in-jokes become karma too.

  3. ++

    Or type it

    Mention the bot and a teammate with a run of pluses: @plusplus @sam ++. Longer runs give more, and -- docks. iOS turning -- into an em dash? Handled.

  4. ±5

    Capped & fair

    Only your strongest reaction on a message counts, and every hit moves the needle by at most ±5, so nobody mints a fortune by stacking emojis or typing +++++++++. Self-awards are denied.

What you get

Live leaderboard

Run /plusplus-leaderboard for the all-time standings. Scoped per workspace, so your numbers stay yours.

Per-channel settings

Choose whether the bot replies in-channel or in a thread, and how much snark it brings, from dry to fully unhinged. Run /plusplus-settings in any channel.

Your emojis, your jokes

Use your own emojis, custom ones included, for the whole workspace or just one channel with /plusplus-settings emoji.

Group karma

Award a whole user group at once. Spread the love (or the blame) across the team.

Multi-workspace

One-click OAuth install. Every workspace is isolated — separate scores, separate settings.

Built to last

A small Go service on DynamoDB. On-demand billing means no idle cost and nothing to babysit.

Commands

👏 🙌 ⭐ 🔥 🏆 / 🙄 👎 🤡 💩 🗑️ React to a message to give or take 1–5 karma. Your strongest reaction counts.
@plusplus @user ++ / -- Award or dock karma by typing. Min 2 symbols, capped at ±5 per message.
/plusplus-leaderboard Show the all-time karma leaderboard for the workspace.
/plusplus-settings reply_mode thread|channel Configure where the bot posts its replies.
/plusplus-settings emoji [workspace] <tier> :emoji: Use your own emoji for a tier (−5 to 5) in this channel. Workspace admins can add workspace to change it everywhere.
